If you have the app

Open Family Meal Mixer, sign in, then go to Settings → Privacy & data → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent: it removes your household's meal plans, recipes, photos, votes, pantry, grocery lists, and public social profile, and deletes your login. There is no recovery window.

Only the household's Manager can do this, and it requires the Manager PIN.

If you no longer have access to the app

Email [CONTACT_EMAIL — to be published before launch] from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete your account and data. We will verify your request and process it manually.

What gets deleted

  • Your account login (email/password)
  • Your household's meal plans, recipes, photos, and pantry
  • Votes, suggestions, and served-meal history
  • Your public social profile and follower/following relationships, if you had one
  • Usage analytics tied to your household

Recipes another household copied from you into their own library before deletion are not affected — they own that copy independently, the same way saving something keeps it if the original is later removed.
